# Support & FAQ — TabCost Pro

## Getting help

- **Bug or unexpected behavior?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/projekta2/tabcost-pro-source/issues) — include your Chrome version and, if possible, steps to reproduce.
- **Feature idea?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/projekta2/tabcost-pro-source/issues) with the `enhancement` label, or check the [Roadmap](#-roadmap) in the main README first — it might already be planned.
- **Something about your Pro license?** Email [hello@projekta2.com](mailto:hello@projekta2.com) with your Gumroad order number.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I activate my Pro license?**
After purchasing on [Gumroad](https://projekta2.gumroad.com/l/tabcost-pro), open the TabCost popup → Settings → paste the license key from your Gumroad receipt email → Activate. Verification happens once, then periodically in the background.

**I closed a tab I didn't want closed. Can I undo it?**
TabCost only closes tabs past your configured grace period, and always leaves the active tab open. There's no undo for closed tabs — this is a Chrome limitation, not something TabCost can control. If this happens often, increase your grace period or add the site to your Pro domain whitelist.

**Can I use my license on more than one computer?**
Yes — one license per OS user profile, with up to 3 deactivate/reactivate cycles per year to cover normal device changes.

**What's the refund policy?**
30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. Email [hello@projekta2.com](mailto:hello@projekta2.com) with your order number.

**Does TabCost work offline?**
Yes. Cost calculation runs entirely locally. The only moment TabCost needs a network connection is license verification for Pro.

**Why does the popup show $0.00 even though I've had tabs open a while?**
Check that your hourly rate is set (Settings) and that the tabs in question are past the grace period (5 min free tier, configurable 1–30 min in Pro).
